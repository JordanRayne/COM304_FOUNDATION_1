# Council Proposal 1
A Proposal to replace all the office PC's with a hot desk solution.
## Explanation of this proposal
The idea foe this proposal is to stop giving workers their own desks and computer but allows everyone to use any free space on a first come first serve bases. This proposal is meant to help reduce space, cost while also making it a more flexible inviroment for different teams

## Technologies and research relevant to this proposal
For hot desking to work it will need lots of cloud storage so people can access their information from any computer. It will also need a booking system so staff are able to reserve a workspace before they come in. Good WiFi and sercurity are needed to protect information on the shared computers. 
## Initial Analysis of the Proposal
This proposal has some benefits aswell as challenges. while it can make the office more flexible and reduse unused desks it can also be a breach of security and comfort.

## Evaluation of the proposal
Hot desking can offer many benefits like saving money, reduce unused space, and give staff more flexibilit. It can also help by allowing people to meet new people which can help with team work. Although their are also some problems like security risks, and no personal workspace but the proposal can be successfull if the coucil upgrades its teck, and creates clear rules.
