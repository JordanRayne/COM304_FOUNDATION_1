# Council Proposal 3

## Explanation of this proposal
This proposal would allow council staff to work from home where they would be able to connect to council sysyems using a VPN. Staff will be able to do their work remotley while also keeping council data safe.

## Technologies and research relevant to this proposal
VPN- Allows staff to securely access coucil systems from home. This keeps information safe

Cloud services- Staff will be able to store and share documents online instead of having to rely on phyisical files that can be easily lost.

Secure Devices- personal or work provided laptops with antivirus and encryption can help protect important data.
